Privacy Policy

Unshaken ("we", "us") publishes short-form Stoic philosophy content to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube. This page explains what little data our integrations touch.

What we collect

When you connect a platform account (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube) to authorize posting, the platform shares only the minimum account identifiers needed to publish content (e.g. a page or channel ID and an access token). We do not collect data from viewers or followers who simply watch or interact with our posts.

How we use it

Account identifiers and tokens are used solely to publish scheduled content to the connected account. They are stored as encrypted secrets in our private automation repository and are never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Data retention

Tokens are kept only as long as the connected account remains active in our posting schedule, and can be revoked at any time from the platform's own account settings.
